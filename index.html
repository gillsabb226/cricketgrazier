About Our Academy

Building Future Cricket Stars ⭐

Welcome to GRAZIER CRICKET ACADEMY - the most professional cricket academy in Patti, Punjab! Located at Cambridge International School, we provide world-class cricket training with experienced coaches.

Our academy is equipped with modern facilities including turf wickets, bowling machines, video analysis, and fitness training programs.

Whether you are a beginner or an advanced player, we have specialized programs for all age groups (8-25 years).
